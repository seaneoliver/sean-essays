# I built 6 AI workers this week. Here's what happened.

*Each handles one job. All run simultaneously. Here's the real impact.*

**I built six specialized AI workers this week.**

Each handles one job. All run simultaneously. Here’s what that actually looks like.

**Weekly Status Updates**

Every Friday I send my manager an update. Priorities, completed work, impact, blockers, questions.

I used to spend 45 minutes writing it. Now I dump my weekly notes into Claude Code and get a draft in 30 seconds.

I still edit it. The AI occasionally misses context and overstates impact. But the structure is there, the bullets are formatted, and I’m shipping updates in 10 minutes instead of an hour.

**Meeting Follow-Up**

I record every meeting. Claude extracts action items, surfaces objections, flags decisions that need documentation.

It doesn’t catch everything. When people speak vaguely or defer decisions, the AI just transcribes the vagueness. But for 80% of meetings, I get a clean summary before the next one starts.

**Manager Sanity Checks**

I built a sub-agent that mimics my manager’s communication style. Before I send a proposal up the chain, I run it past the simulation.

It catches when I’m too technical, too vague, or asking for something without explaining the why. Not perfect - my real manager brings context the AI doesn’t have - but it eliminates obvious mistakes.

**Where It Doesn’t Work**

Strategy conversations. Persuading stakeholders. Reading the room in a tense meeting.

AI can’t replace judgment. It doesn’t know which battles matter or when to push back. It summarizes what was said, not what wasn’t.

**What This Actually Means**

I’m not doing different work. I’m doing the same job with less friction.

The repeatable stuff, formatting, extracting, drafting, fact-checking- happens in seconds. That gives me more time for the work that requires me.

The gap forming isn’t AI users vs non-users. It’s between people who spot the repeatable patterns in their work and those who don’t.

If you spend 30 minutes every week formatting the same type of update, that’s a pattern. If you manually copy-paste action items from transcripts, that’s a pattern.

AI doesn’t give you superpowers. It gives you back the time you were wasting on repetitive cognitive work.
