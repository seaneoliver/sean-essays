# Steal My 3-Step Process to Identify Your Most Helpful Job Search Contacts

*Or...what I've been up to for the last 12 months*

**Ever wondered why some people land jobs through their network effortlessly?**

My 13-month job hunt taught me the secret. During that search, I learned the difference between words and actions. I’m sharing a potent 3 step process that helped me identify the most helpful contacts and uncover hidden opportunities.

## The Power of Your Network

**Tapping into your network can feel overwhelming and scary when you're on the job hunt.**

After hitting a career snag, *\*cough\* layoff \*cough\*,* I realized the goldmine I had in my network. Yet, the real trick was sifting through it to find the genuine gems - the people who would do more than just nod along but give me a leg up in my job quest.

## Identifying Your True Advocates

Here’s the million-dollar question: "Who's really got my back?" It might surprise you that it's not always the loudest cheerleaders. Instead, it's often those who quietly step up with real, tangible help. Figuring out who those folks are is key to turning the job search tide in your favor.

## My 3-Step Game Plan

Ready for the big reveal? Here’s how you sort your fan club from your true allies:

Step 1. Keep your network in the loop with regular, no-fluff updates on your job search efforts.

Step 2. Notice who comes through with action - introductions, tips, and even valuable words of wisdom and support.

Step 3. Nurture relationships with your most helpful contacts and continue to provide them with updates and opportunities to assist you.

They’re the ones that count. I started with a monthly email. Then I moved to bi-weekly once a contact told me “Oh, I thought you’d gotten hired long ago”.

This method pinpointed my most helpful contacts and led me straight to otherwise hidden opportunities.

## Key Takeaways

- Your network is your greatest asset during a job search

- Identifying your most helpful contacts is crucial to your success

- Send regular updates, pay attention to actionable support, and nurture relationships

- Adopt this 3-step process to not just transform your job search but forge deeper, more meaningful professional connections.

Implementing this proven 3-step process will unlock the full potential of your network and accelerate your path to your next career opportunity. Start today and watch your job search transform before your eyes!
