# The AI system that transformed my performance review

*Find what works, then use AI to execute it consistently. The compounding effect is real.*

I built it over four months.

The result? My best performance review in six-plus years at Microsoft.

The feedback wasn’t about fixing shortcomings, it was about doubling down on what’s working and building on opportunities.

That didn’t happen by accident.\
\
From July to November, I built a system. I gathered performance criteria for my role and the next level up. I documented leadership behaviors, my job description, and direct conversations with my managers about what success looks like in their eyes.

Then I used AI to translate my activities into their language. Their metaphors. Their structure.

**Use AI to speak your manager’s language.**

I created an agent that simulated my manager’s point of view. I fed it their writing style, their priorities, their frameworks for thinking about impact.

When I drafted my status updates, I ran them through this agent first. The result? Updates that landed exactly how they needed to land.

When you present your impact using their framework, there’s very little to reject.

**Create a weekly execution plan.**

I mapped all available channels - deliverables, emails, meetings, Teams messages - to specific opportunities to demonstrate impact.

Every week, I had a clear plan to take action across all fronts. Not random activity. Strategic visibility.

**Treat AI like a craftsman’s tool.**

Most people scan AI-summarized meetings for their name. They skim recaps for action items.

Few are building repeatable systems that create actual leverage.

Find what works, then use AI to execute it consistently. Week after week. The compounding effect is real.

The result? My review feedback was framed as opportunities to build on, not problems to fix.

The language shifted from “you need to address this” to “here’s what you could do more of.”

That shift matters. It changes how you’re positioned for the next level. It changes what opportunities come your way.

Your manager has a mental model of what success looks like. AI can help you deliver exactly that. Not by gaming the system but by removing the translation friction between your work and their evaluation framework.

Make it easy for your manager to see your impact.
