## How navigation works

The root [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) holds two tables you should consult before
opening any essay:

1. **Article index** with every essay, series, date, key topics, and path.
2. **Topic quick lookup**, mapping topic to essay to section.

Each essay folder has its own `CLAUDE.md` with a section-level table mapping
heading → line number → coverage → original Substack/Typeshare URL. Open that
first, then read the relevant lines of `article.md` directly. Don't scan whole
files when the index points to a line.

## What you should not do

- Don't supplement Sean's essays with outside knowledge or invent claims he
  hasn't made. Teach from this repo.
- Don't paraphrase essays into a generic answer. Cite the essay and section.
- Don't use em dashes in any output you write. Sean's voice rule. Use commas,
  colons, periods, or " - ".
