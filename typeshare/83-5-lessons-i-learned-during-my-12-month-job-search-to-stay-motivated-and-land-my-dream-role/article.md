# 5 Lessons I Learned During My 12-Month Job Search to Stay Motivated and Land My Dream Role

**Did you know that the average job search lasts 5 months?**\
\
Mine lasted 12. That's an entire year of facing rejection, doubting myself, and wondering if I'd ever find my dream job. But I refused to give up, and along the way, I learned some invaluable lessons about staying motivated.\
\
**Listen up if you're a job seeker feeling the weight of a lengthy search.**\
\
**The five pieces of advice I wish I could give my past self apply to you, too:**\
\
1. Celebrate your small wins\
2. Focus on what you can control\
3. Surround yourself with supportive people\
4. Practice self-care\
5. Remember your worth\
\
Applying these strategies will help you stay motivated and become a stronger, more resilient job seeker - exactly what you need to be to land your next role. So, what are you waiting for? Start putting these five pieces of advice into action today.\
\
**Your future you will thank you.**
