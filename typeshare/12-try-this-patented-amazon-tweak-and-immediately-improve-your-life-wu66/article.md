# Try this patented Amazon tweak and immediately improve your life

**Have you noticed how easy Amazon makes it to buy stuff?**

Back in the day, buying stuff online was a hassle. 70% of carts were abandoned. You'd have to enter your Full name, mailing address, payment info, BILLING ADDRESS DO I EVEN WANT THIS?!

**It was a lot of effort.**

Amazon is in the business of selling, and 20+ years ago create 1-Click buying. This change made buying easier by removing friction. You can steal this change and apply it to your life today.

## Where can you remove friction from your life?

Few people like chores. My mom would clean the entire house blasting Marvin Gaye and Motown hits. Great tunes make light work. A small addition of fun can make the work easier.

**Dishes can pile up.**

But not when you wash them immediately when you are done. A simple rule to wash it now prevents Mt. Rushmore dirty dish scenarios.

**Getting ready to work out is a drag.**

But if the night before I make sure to:

- Fill water bottle

- Set out workout clothes

Then I can stumble into a workout with little fuss.

**Where can you remove friction in your life?**

Think of three things that are hard for you. Then think of three ways you can be a friend to your future self, and make it easier on you.

**These little tweaks compound.**

Before you know it you'll be less stressed. Less stress makes you more productive. When you're more productive, what else will you be able to accompliish?
