# Are you still lying to everyone?

I sabotaged my life with one diabolical daily lie.

I told this lie when planning dinner with friends. I told it when job hunting. Even when planning lazy Sundays.

The lie was I didn't have a preference and I was open to whatever.

Clarity is kindness

If you don't start sharing your preferences when asked, you'll destroy your life.

When you dodge your responsibility of deciding, you place the burden on everyone else. Not wanting to "impose" seems like graciousness. You may even be 100% genuinely open to any potential option.

When someone asks you for your preference, tell them.

Get respect, give respect

Someone showing you consideration is an act of respect.

If someone asks you:

What you'd like to eat

What you'd like to watch

Where you'd like to work

Tell them the truth. If you are open to any instance of the above, your decision only leads to a positive result. Sharing your preference respects both the relationship and the individual.

Help move the interaction forward

When folks ask your preference, they are asking for help.

Typically they are trying to narrow something down to fewer options. Pizza or Thai? What would you like to eat?

Sharing what you specifically want helps narrow down the universe of "whatever".

Share the cost

A study from Cornell University estimated the average adult makes about 35,000 decisions each day.

When you share a solicited preference, you are doing your part to lighten someone else cognitive load. As yourself right now, do you want to guess what your child/friend/partner/parent would like to drink right now? Or would you rather know?

Don't add to everyone else's work with your lie of having no preference.

Tell the truth and watch your life immediately improve.
