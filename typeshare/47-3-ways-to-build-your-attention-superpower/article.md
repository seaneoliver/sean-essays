# 3 Ways To Build Your Attention Superpower

**Connecting the dots is key, but it requires focused attention.**\
Here are some tips to help you get started:

### 1. Focus on the big picture and connect it to your daily work.

See how your daily work fits into the larger goals of the company or project.

Understand how the different pieces of the puzzle fit together. Like a map, work to fill in spaces that are less familiar to you.

Use this perspective to identify where you can make the most impact.

### 2. Look for patterns and trends to identify potential issues or opportunities.

Analyze data to see if there are any patterns or trends that can inform decisions.

Pay attention to what customers are saying about your products or services.

Use this information to identify potential issues or opportunities for growth.

### 3. Be proactive and use your insights to drive positive change.

Don't just identify issues or opportunities, take action to address them.

Use your insights to inform decisions and drive positive change.

Be willing to take risks and try new things to achieve your goals.

By following these tips, you can start connecting the dots and uncover new opportunities for growth and success. Remember, it takes a little effort and intentionality, but the payoff is worth it.\
**Don't be afraid to think big and make connections that others may not see.**
