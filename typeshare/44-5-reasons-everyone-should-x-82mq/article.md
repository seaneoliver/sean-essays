# 5 Reasons Everyone Should Start A Work Newsletter

**I am a big believer that writing has the power to change your life.**

I have been writing newsletters for my friends, colleagues, and mentors for over 5 years, and since that time I have made stronger connections with influential leaders in my company, discovered new professional opportunities, and increased my visibility in an authentic way. Doing this has also helped me find an engaging community of people, make new friends, and improve myself in ways I otherwise never would have. I recommend everyone start writing a newsletter of their own.

## And I have 5 specific reasons why:

**Reason \#1: Increase your visibility.** People are busy, just creating regular content is something I'd wager none of your coworkers do.

**Reason \#2: Create serendipity.** Folks will reply randomly, but always in response to YOU.

**Reason \#3: Develop a POV.** It could be the format or the subject, but you will build a voice unique to you.

**Reason \#4: Build relationships.** You can only talk about work so much. This allows you to be more than one-note.

**Reason \#5: Adding value.** Education, entertainment, provoking thought. All of these are better than asking for that TPS report.

**If you start writing a work newsletter, let me know - I'd love to connect with you (and answer any questions you have getting started!).**
