# What I Learned After 7 Essays

**In the last week, I've written more words than I have all year.**

I've learned about headlines, writing flow, and new types of writing. All this has happened in public, with lots of feedback. That short feedback loop & its benefits has been an incredible surprise. The data tells the tale. Over 8,000 impressions on these essays.

> **What essay got the most impressions?**

*3 Steps You Probably Missed To Crush Toxic Contacts*

This won with 1086 impressions on your eyeballs. This is 2x the number of visits I get on my side in a month. The power of Twitter and its reach shines here.

> **What essay got the most likes?**

*How To Get Almost Anything You Want In Life*

This felt good. It's a message I try to drill into my mentees each year. I'm glad 28 folks smashed that like button.

> **What essay generated the most conversation?**

*10 Things I Can't Live Without*

This was fun. I think folks like sharing their own suggestions and riffing off of mine. It was a fun break and I learned a lot about new people.

> **What surprised me the most?**

I was surprised at how wrong I've been about what is popular or engaging. It will allow me to dial in and create content that people want.

For this week, I'm planning to write better tweets. They deserve first-class treatment.

**Thank you reader, for the lessons.**
