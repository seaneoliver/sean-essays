# The Insta-Pot Method To Harvest Ideas From Books

This method will help you remember more of what you read.

I've been there. I've read book after book and totally forgotten what I've read. What felt solid at the moment was only a fleeting engagement with the text.

You can do better.

### It starts with my Kindle

Yes, paper books have their charm. What they don't allow you to do is highlight text & make notes that can be digitally moved to other services. That's critical to this process. We make our notes and highlights digitally.

### Next, highlights are pushed to Readwise

Readwise is an amazing tool for surfacing your highlights. It's a simple way to add spaced repetition to your reading. Our use here is slightly different. We use Readwise to send our highlights to Roam.

### Roam is where the work happens

That work is progressive summarization. Popularized by Tiago Forte, this is where we apply the pressure of progressive summarization. The best passages are bolded, then the best of those are highlighted.

### The final step is the most valuable.

**This is where your thoughts collide with the text.**

Using the best of the best of your highlights, you are going to summarize and write ideas in your own words. Things you agree with. Things you found interesting.

**Most importantly write what captured your attention.**

Those ideas represent the distillation of what you've extracted from a book or article. Capture these ideas, these notes in a place you can reference and connect them to other books and thoughts.

This synthesis is the valuable crop of your effort.

**Watch your insight and the quality of your ideas grow.**
