# You are tanking your job search results with your crappy networking

**Failing to connect is crippling your job search.**

Strong connections open doors.

It's not who you know; it's who knows you

## Most jobs are filled through networking

In my last three Fortune 500 jobs, each hiring manager already knew me, either from working together or from many conversations beforehand.

## Networking unlocks 70% of hidden jobs and boosts your odds of getting hired

The Wall Street Journal reports 85% of jobs are filled through networking.

Referrals are 50% more likely to move past the interview screen.

## Five ways to network with purpose during your job search

1. Be curious: Ask questions they can uniquely answer

2. Be strategic: Target people working in roles you want

3. Be authentic: Don't pitch, start real conversations

4. Be consistent: Follow up, don't just try to get hired in one shot

5. Use social: Show up regularly in places with eyeballs

When you follow these steps over time, you will increase the number of people who know who you are, what you're doing, and have heard from you recently. This keeps you recent & relevant when an opportunity arises.

**People can't recommend you if they've forgotten you.**

The easiest way to get hired at a place is to have someone on the inside who wants you to work there.
