# 15 Remote Work Productivity Tips

**Working from home requires you to create a productive environment.** These are 15 tips that may help you improve your work-from-home experience. What would you add?

**What would you remove?**

### Tasks

- Keep your tasks all in one place in a productivity tool(I use Todoist).

&nbsp;

- Break tasks that are too big down into bite-sized chunks

&nbsp;

- Put your must-do events & actions on your calendar

&nbsp;

- Listen to 1 lyric-free song on repeat

&nbsp;

- Use the Pomodoro technique

&nbsp;

- Review your tasks regularly

&nbsp;

- Respect your energy levels

&nbsp;

- Focus on 1 thing at a time

### Health

- Eat some fresh fruit

&nbsp;

- Drink enough water

&nbsp;

- Get sufficient sleep

&nbsp;

- Drink Green Tea

&nbsp;

- Take breaks

&nbsp;

- Take a walk

### Summary

The building blocks of your best work-from-home experience are there for you to remix as you wish.
