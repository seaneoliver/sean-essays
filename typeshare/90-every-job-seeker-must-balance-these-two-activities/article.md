# Every Job Seeker Must Balance These Two Activities

A recent study highlighted the significant advantage of referrals in job applications: **those with referrals had a 50% chance of advancing past the initial resume review, starkly higher than the 12% odds for other external candidates.**

**Activity 1-Cold Applications:** Often, job seekers rely solely on cold applications to online postings.

This method, while straightforward, is also the most competitive. Some services automate applications, submitting up to 200 monthly, but this spray-and-pray strategy may not align with focused job search goals.

**Activity 2-Networking:** Remember, people hire people.

Engaging directly with decision-makers and securing human referrals dramatically increases your chances of receiving a job offer. Conversations with these key individuals are crucial.

**Strategy Adjustment:** Steve Dalton, the author of The 2-Hour Job Search, advocates for a pragmatic approach: **if cold applications yield a high success rate, continue; if not, and the interview to application ratio drops below 5%, shift to a networking-first strategy.**

This adjustment enhances your likelihood of success by connecting you directly with those who have hiring power.

**There's no one-size-fits-all solution to job searching.**

By strategically balancing cold applications and networking, and focusing efforts where they yield the best returns, you can significantly improve your job search outcomes. Adapting your approach based on results allows you to develop a personalized and effective job search strategy.
