# 3 Rules For Busy Professionals To Live A Results-Filled Life You Can Be Proud Of Each Day

Over the past 10+ years, I have:

- Connected with best-selling authors

&nbsp;

- Worked at 3 Fortune 500 companies

&nbsp;

- Spoken with industry leaders

And all of these things have taught me a tremendous amount about what matters in life, and what doesn’t. (Plot twist: I don't care about many of the things I used to care about anymore. And things I did not care very much about are now my top priorities.)

These are my new 3 rules for life:

1. **Own your output**

We can't control outcomes. There are no guarantees. We can take responsibility for our output.

2. **Velocity rules**

Velocity is speed +direction. When you intentionally move in a direction at your best speed, that's the space where you gain valuable experience.

3. **Curate your calendar**

Your calendar filled with your most important tasks for that day is a decision about what, when, and where you are going to put your effort(see rule 2).\
\
**Consistently applied over time, these 3 rules create days that add up to significant change.**
