# Why Does This Regular Guy Create Content?

**Why does a regular guy like me create content online?**

A friend asked me why do all these things online? Why all the tweets, essays, newsletter, YouTube? What's it for?

It's a great question that caught me by surprise.

*So here's why I create content.*

### I make content to build my skills.

If you've been paying attention, content is created. It's made. The stuff we engage with daily is the result of someone making it.

**I wanted to build the skills to write, film, and otherwise capture attention.**

### I create content to build an audience.

Getting good at winning the attention game is an underrated skill. Capturing, holding, and directing attention will set you apart from everyone else.

> "The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is now." - Proverb

**It would be a mistake to wait until I NEED the skill to start building it.**

### I create content to build a personal monopoly.

This comes from David Perrell. I wanted to take my experience + my skills & interests, + my personality to create a body of work unique to me. That's a protective moat with no competition.

**No competition= opportunity.**

Working in public, I've noticed a change in how people interact with me around my interests. Folks I haven't spoken to in ages referencing something I wrote: friends sharing links and introductions to like-minded people(Thanks, Nate!).

**Every public asset you create is a sprinkle of serendipity.**

My friend asked me why I create content because he doesn't know many people who put in that work. He's right. Most people are content to just consume.

**I'd rather build the leverage from cooking up stuff myself.**

What about you?
