# The 3 Biggest Mistakes People Make When Networking  

## Mistake 1: Treating it like an event

**Networking isn't a one-time effort.**

Consistency is key. Strong networks are built over time through consistent actions that grow, support, and leverage your connections.

**Proactively nurture your network, not just when you need help, to avoid a common rookie mistake.**

## Mistake 2: Neglecting to grow it

**The reality is that your network is either growing or shrinking.**

If you don't continually keep an eye open for people with new perspectives, experiences, and interests you risk stagnation. That stagnation creates an echo chamber. A group of folks doing, saying, and thinking the same old things.

**Actively seek diverse perspectives and experiences to enrich and grow your network.**

## Mistake 3: Failing to support your network

**A network is a community of mutual support, not just a resource for personal gain.**

The mistake people make is forgetting they are a part of existing networks. This means people will look to you for help. Think about how you are supporting the networks you're in.

**Make sure you're not only seeking help but also actively offering it.**
