# 3 Questions I Asked Myself Every Day for 8+ Years

**I always thought journaling was dumb.**

Why on earth would I want to write down my secret hopes and dreams? Why trust that to those flimsy little locks? I just didn't get it.

**That all changed when I found an email service that let me summarize my day by simply replying to an email.**

That started my journaling habit. Each day I'd write out a couple of sentences about my day and it would surface a random day to remind me of how things were.

**I became a journaling fan.**

Since then, I've answered three prompts each morning.

### Prompt 1 "I'm grateful for"

This helps me focus on what is good in my life. There is always something to be grateful for. Looking back over the years it's interesting for me to see what changes and what doesn't.

### Prompt 2 "Results for today"

We aren't making any more time. As a firm believer in "No zero days", this prompt focuses me on what result I want from it. I don't always make the mark.

**I do make the effort.**

### Prompt 3 "What would make today great"

This is my favorite prompt.

With a focus on the positive, it helps keep my mind open for opportunities. The world is horrible. The world is amazing.

**It all depends on your focus.**

With 8+ years of data on my mindset, it's nice to see evidence of growth.

**What can you start learning from yourself today?**
