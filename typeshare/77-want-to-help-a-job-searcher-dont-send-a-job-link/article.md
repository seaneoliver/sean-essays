# Want To Help A Job Searcher? Don't Send A Job Link

## **More than 240,000 jobs were lost in the tech industry in 2023.**

Those people are looking for work. If you want to help any of them, don't send them a link to a job posting you saw. Instead, send them a lotto ticket. The average corporate job posting receives over 250 applicants.

## **Of those applicants, four to six will be interviewed.**

That's a hair more than 2%, or 98% of people never getting an interview. Most lotto scratch-off tickets have winning odds of 1 in 2.18.

## **Scratchers pay out almost 50% of the time!**

Sure, lotto tickets cost money. We are talking about investing in people you want to see win! While there are no guarantees in life, there are wastes of time. Alternatively, consider gifting a roulette spin(always bet on black.)

## The odds of betting on black on a US roulette table are typically 47.37% in American roulette, which translates to a 1:1 payout.

That's 45% better than applying online!
