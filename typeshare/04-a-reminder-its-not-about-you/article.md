# A Reminder: It's Not About You

Your opinion doesn't matter.

It's hard to hear. It's also true. The only thing unique about your opinion is that well, it's yours.

**That doesn't mean it's important.**

### What is important is being of service to others.

That could mean a few things:

- Being informative

&nbsp;

- Being entertaining

&nbsp;

- All up being helpful

## Anything else is ego

If it's not about serving something or someone outside of you, that's self-centeredness. That's fine. Just see it for what it is.

There are lots of ways to engage with the world.

I'm not here to tell you what to do. Just a reminder. You are the center of your world, not THE world.

**Accept this truth and go forward with power.**

### You can use ego for good

You can start with yourself. Apply a selfish interest in improving anyone, everyone, everything around you for good.

Through your actions, you can be the agent of change. The literal hero.

### **People will ask, who did this marvelous work?**

They will look for the source of this substance. The source of real impact. It could be you.
