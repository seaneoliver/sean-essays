# 1 Productivity Lesson For Modern Workers To Get More Done Today

**Take action.**

Do something. Anything. Be proactive.

What do all my favorite movies have in common? A villain who's the real game-changer.

I love the Mission Impossible movie franchise. The plots are simple. Get our hero up a tree, throw rocks at them, and get them down eventually.

**It's a tried-and-true formula, not just for Mission Impossible but for storytelling itself.**

Heroes get chased, forced, and cornered up that tree. They're not the instigators; they're reactive.

**Only the villains are proactive.**

The villain takes charge, forcing the hero into a corner. Heroes merely respond to the conditions villains orchestrate. So why am I talking about villains in a post on productivity? Because their actions influence my mindset.

**Villains lead; heroes follow.**

That's the lesson. I've said it before, and it's worth repeating: villains act, heroes react. When I'm stuck, I look for ways to be proactive.

**A wrong move is better than no move at all.**

And you can't be stuck and take action simultaneously. So, I can ask myself how a villain would approach this situation. How would they reach this goal?

**That's typically enough to get me unstuck and move forward.**

Adopt a villain's mindset. It's my favorite hack for breaking through a rut.
