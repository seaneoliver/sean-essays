# The 10-Week Strategy That Turned Job Hunting Into Valuable Insight

Job hunting is a drag.

But despite being one of the hardest things in the world, most people have no clue how they did what they did. Honestly, neither did I. So, I took it upon myself to moderate a Job Search Council to study exactly how - and see if I could find a repeatable framework I could use myself to have better success in the job market.

20 hours later, here’s what I learned:

**Quick Stats About Job Referrals**

Here are a few interesting data points to give you context, in case you're unfamiliar with professional networking:

- 75% of people apply online for jobs

- 20% of open jobs are advertised

- 80% of the remaining jobs are filled by referral or word-of-mouth

Needless to say, the best way to get a new job isn't applying online.

So... what can we learn?

**Phyl Terry’s 5-Step Framework For Landing Your Dream Job**

From my research, all their success can be boiled down to a very simple process:

1. Form a Job Search Council

2. Conduct a listening tour

3. Form your candidate-market fit

4. Create a job mission & OKRs

5. Negotiate for budget, resources, and support

Pair this framework with an unmatched work ethic, and you get a potent combination for beating your competition.
