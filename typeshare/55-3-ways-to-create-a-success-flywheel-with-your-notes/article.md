# 3 Ways to Create a Success Flywheel With Your Notes

**Your notes can be a superpower for your success; here's how.**

The following three practices will pave the way for creating a record of growth and wins:

1. **Noting Positive Feedback:** Positive feedback serves as external recognition of our accomplishments. Jotting these moments down provides a tangible reminder of the value we bring and the impact of our work. This practice is invaluable, especially in moments when self-doubt creeps in.

2. **Documenting Goal Progress:** Keeping a record of progress on set goals enables us to see how far we've come, reinforcing our self-reliance and motivating us to press on. Noting milestones and challenges you've beat provides a real-time story of our journey towards your goals.

3. **Recording Wins:** Big or small, every win matters. Documenting successes, whether they're major project completions or smaller, daily victories, helps build a positive mindset and a habit of celebrating success. This practice also you in building a portfolio of impact.

**By adopting these note-taking practices, you'll create a self-reinforcing cycle of success.** Positive feedback will drive motivation, visible goal progress keeps you focused, and documenting wins breeds a success-oriented mindset.

**This written archive will serve not only as a powerful self-motivation tool but also as an undeniable showcase of your achievements.**
