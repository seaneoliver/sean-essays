# Master the Art of Documenting Your Wins

**With 8 billion people on the planet, no one is closer to your success than you.**

You do amazing work. You just do a terrible job of keeping track.

**Here are 3 reasons why you should make notes on your wins a weekly habit.**

### Reason \#1

**Success gives you confidence:** Every win, big or small, boosts your self-image. It shows you're capable and encourages you to take on bigger challenges. From putting on pants, to crafting a fantastic proposal, it all adds up.

**Write it down.**

### Reason \#2

**It shows your growth:** Keeping a record of your victories is like keeping a progress report. It helps you see how far you've come and can give you a morale boost when things get tough.

**It all adds up.**

### Reason \#3

**It helps others see your value:** When you keep track of your wins, you also improve your ability to let others know about them. It's not about bragging - it's about showing others what you're capable of. Remember, what is easy and effortless for you may be difficult and dreadful for others.

**Ensure your success by documenting your achievements on a weekly basis, providing yourself with 52 accomplishments to reflect on each year.**
