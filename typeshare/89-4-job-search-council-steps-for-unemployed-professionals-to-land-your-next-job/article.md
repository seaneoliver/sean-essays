# 4 Job Search Council Steps For Unemployed Professionals To Land Your Next Job

**Job hunting can be one of the most challenging experiences, and many people struggle to understand how they successfully navigate the process**.

I moderated a job search council to gain insights and develop a repeatable framework for job search success. The research revealed some interesting statistics: 75% of people apply online for jobs, only 20% of open jobs are advertised, and 80% of the remaining jobs are filled by referral or word-of-mouth. This highlights the importance of professional networking over relying solely on online applications.

**The Job Search Council's success stories can be distilled into Phyl Terry's Framework For Landing Your Dream Job.**

Begin by forming or joining a Job Search Council - a group of peers and mentors who support each other through structured feedback and shared resources. The second step involves forming your candidate-market fit and understanding how your skills and experiences align with the market's needs.

**Next, create a job mission and OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) to guide your search and keep you focused.** Finally, negotiate for the budget, resources, and support to ensure you have the necessary tools and backing to succeed in your job search. By combining this framework with a strong work ethic, job seekers can develop a powerful strategy for outperforming their competition in the job market.

**The key takeaway is that professional networking and a structured approach to job searching are more effective than relying solely on online applications.** Implementing Phyl Terry's Framework can help job seekers navigate the challenging process and increase their chances of landing their dream job.
