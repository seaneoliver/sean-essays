# 3 Books I'm Reading Right Now To Get Better At Job Searching

# What Color Is Your Parachute

So, here are 3 books I'm reading - specifically to help me:

- Improve my marketing materials

- Improve my networking

- Improve my interviews

# Book \#1: The Ultimate Guide to Getting A PM Job by Aakash Gupta

I picked up this book because the job search game has changed.

So far, my biggest takeaways are to treat your search like enterprise sales, lists are your friend, and keep your network updated.

# Book \#2: The 2-Hour Job Search by Steve Dalton

This book has taught me a lot about conducting a strategic job search.

Some golden nuggets so far:

- List your target companies

- Identify connections at those companies

- Conduct cold networking in a consistent manner

# Book \#3: What Color Is Your Parachute by Richard N. Bolles

Finally, I'd recommend What Color Is Your Parachute to anyone who wants to clarify their dream job.

This book has taught me a lot about clarifying what I'm good at, detailing what I like doing, and listing the values most important to me in my next role..
