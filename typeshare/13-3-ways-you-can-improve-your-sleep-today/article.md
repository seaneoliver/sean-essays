# 3 ways you can improve your sleep today

**These three changes have dramatically improved the quality of my sleep.**

If you've issues with:

- Light sleeping

&nbsp;

- Feeling exhausted

&nbsp;

- Needing naps daily to survive

then keep reading for changes that helped me get a better night's sleep.

### With these changes, you can improve your sleep.

### 1. Start tracking your sleep

**What you measure, you master.**

I have used my Apple watch to track my sleep since 2017. Since 2020 I've used the Oura ring for measuring the quality of my sleep. The device doesn't matter as much as getting a consistent measure of your sleep situation.

### 2. Fixed wake-up time

**Setting a consistent wake-up time every day helped me establish a routine before making any other changes.**

With a foundation of a consistent wake-up time, I could better understand my sleep efficiency. Sleep efficiency is the time you are asleep relative to the total time you are in bed. Before setting a consistent wake-up time, my sleep efficiency was 80% or below.

**Now I consistently get about 85% sleep efficiency.**

### 3. Block blue light

**I know, blue light-blocking glasses look corny.**

Blue light from screens and indoor lights hurts your body's production of melatonin, the sleep hormone. Blocking blue light lets your body naturally cycle down to a rest state, getting you sleepy. So I wear them 2 hours before bedtime.

**This change improved my sleep scores improved 5%, and I get to look like Cyclops from the X-Men.**

Sleep is something you can improve with deliberate effort. So what change can you make today to improve your sleep situation?
