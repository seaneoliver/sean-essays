# 5 Ways To Make Meaningful Connections

**Did you know that 85% of jobs are filled through networking?**

Most career opportunities never even hit the job boards - they're snatched up through connections. This sobering fact should be a wake-up call: It's not the size of your network that matters, but the strength of your relationships within it.

**Now, let's link this to you, the modern job seeker.**

You're savvy, you're on LinkedIn, but are you leveraging the power of your network? Or are you just collecting names like digital Pokemon?

**I'm sharing five actionable tips that turned my job search from a shot in the dark into a targeted mission:**

Tip 1. Engage with your contacts about more than just jobs. Share updates, offer help, and start conversations like normal human beings.

Tip 2. Schedule regular catch-ups to stay updated on each other's professional lives.

Tip 3. Give more than you take. Recommend others, support their goals, and provide genuine value

Tip 4. Remember details and follow up. A personal touch goes a long way. Those kids have names

Tip 5. Accept that relationships are built over time, and don't fall into the trap of giving-to-get

And for the punchline? \
**It's not who you know or what you know, but who knows you AND holds you in high regard.**\
Make your network an active ecosystem of mutual support and opportunity.

So, who will you genuinely connect with today?
