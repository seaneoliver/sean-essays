# Improve Your Career By Avoiding These 3 Mistakes

**In the corporate world, too much employee experience is wasted.**

It's an issue many overlook, but the lost potential is massive.

**The mistakes lie in three areas: creating knowledge, capturing it, and communicating it.**

### Mistake \#1 Not creating knowledge

Employees tackle tasks and solve problems every day. These experiences are perfect for creating knowledge. But we often fail to stop, think, and convert our experiences into useful knowledge.

### Mistake \#2 Not capturing knowledge

Next, we need to capture this knowledge. When you have an insight, write it down. If you don't, it's likely to get lost in the shuffle of daily work.

**Find a way to record these thoughts that work for you, whether that's an app or good old-fashioned notecards.**

### Mistake \#3 Not sharing knowledge

Knowledge isn't worth much if it's not shared. You have to communicate what you've learned with your team and even your superiors. This way, everyone benefits from your experiences.

**Sadly, many workplaces have become a battleground for status, rather than a place for growth and learning.**

This needs to change. Start valuing your experiences, learn from them, and share them. Don't play status games, play the growth game.

**Your experiences, when used right, can drive success for everyone.**
