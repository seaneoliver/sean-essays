#  I Analysed 22 Films And Here's What I Found 

**You're probably looking at your content the wrong way.**

You're thinking too small.

**You can learn a lesson on content creation from the Marvel Cinematic Universe(MCU).**

You don't have to like comics or movies. There is much to learn from 23 total films spanning 10 years. Often imitated, never replicated. The MCU's success was significant.

*This suite of interconnected stories grossed a total of \$22,557,315,232 worldwide.*

Not every single movie was a masterpiece. Not every movie was a blockbuster like my favorite, Black Panther(released on my birthday). Every movie contributed to a single, sweeping storyline.

> Mr. Stark, you've become part of a bigger universe. -Nick Fury

**Do the same with your content.**

Do you have a video, thread, or essay that didn't perform well? No problem, use that as fuel for a better one. Got a topic that doesn't get traction? Make a round-up of the worst material.

**Use your failures to sweeten your inevitable success.**

You know what to do when you discover something that is a success. Make a sequel. Bigger, louder, stronger.

**Stick to your message while giving people what they want.**

And remember your villains. Let out a controversial opinion. Float a hot take.

**Give people something to loathe, not just something to love.**

Just remember that it's all a piece of a larger universe. Everything you create. Every idea you share.

**You're building your own unique cinematic universe.**
