# Ditch This Dangerous Emotion

Hope is one of the most dangerous of emotions.

Hope is the cotton candy of belief. It tastes great. Cheap.

It doesn't last.

Luck is not a factor. Hope is not a strategy. Fear is not an option. -James Cameron

Most people miss how dangerous hope is. It slipped my notice for years. Then I noticed that Hope is:

Weak

A wish

A feeling that requires no action from us

And it has a negative effect on ourselves and others.

There's an alternative to the dumpster fire of hope.

That alternative is Faith.

How is Faith superior to Hope? Your Sage Sean is glad you asked. Faith is belief backed by action.

Faith = Belief + Action.

There's a text message I hate. It will say "Hope you’re well". Do you know what's missing in that message?

Asking me how I’m doing.

Do you see the difference? Hope requires no action. Shoot, I could HOPE that this essay hits home with you.

As my mom would say “Hope in one hand and s#it in the other and let’s see which gets full first.”

Faith is doing what you can, where you are, with what you have.

Faith is taking an umbrella. Hope is wishing it won't rain. Hope is...hoping your friend is doing well.

Faith is asking them how they are.

You can hope to win the lottery. Or you can have faith that if you stay on your grind, something will give. Faith is doing the reps.

Hope costs nothing.

It requires no action from us. It’s “thoughts and prayers” pointed inward. Faith has a price.

Faith costs your effort, attention, and time.

In return, you and your loved ones will get real results.
