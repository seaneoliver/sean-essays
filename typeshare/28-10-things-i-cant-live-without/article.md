# 10 Things I Can't Live Without 

**In 400 days working from home, I learned what 10 things I absolutely can't live without.**

This is that list.

**1. Bombas Socks**

They are as comfy as they are expensive. One of my favorite surprise gifts to send.

**2. Spotify's discover playlist**

I've been delighted every single week with new suggested music from the sorcerers at Spotify

**3. Wireless noise-canceling headphones**

Shhhhh.

**4. Kitchen Timer**

A lo-fi productivity tool that keeps me tidying up in 5-minute intervals.

**5. My Canon mirrorless camera**

Key piece of kit for making my YouTube videos and work video calls.

**6. TheraGun**

Sitting for long periods of time can be a pain. This massager really hits the spot. \*groan\*

**7. Nespresso machine**

This surprised me. But it is vastly superior to that Keurig crap. Don't @ me on this.

**8. Quality office chair**

This is such a game-changer. Give your sitting parts the upgrade it deserves.

**9. Hummingbirb feeder**

A little bit of nature from my fast feathered friends. A welcome distraction for days spent staring at screens.

**10. Nachos** with 25% Cool Ranch Doritos and 60% Tortilla chips, and 15% Fritos

Try that ratio and thank me later.

What about you? **Reply to this with ONE must-include item you've discovered in the last year.**
