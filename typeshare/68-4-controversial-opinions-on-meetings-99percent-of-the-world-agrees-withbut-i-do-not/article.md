# 4 Controversial Opinions On Meetings 99% Of The World Agrees With - But I Do Not

I have had an interest in productivity for a long time.

In the past 15 years, I have heard many strong opinions about meetings. At first, I agreed with them. But as I learned and educated myself more, I began to disagree. I started to think these beliefs became popular because so many people agreed.

**So, I have a controversial opinion I’d like to share on meetings after 10,000 hours immersing myself in the subject:**

## **1. Meetings are a universal solution**

I’m not saying meetings are bad.

I’m saying meetings are misused.

## **2. Most meetings are the solution to "too many mails"**

Contrary to popular belief, "It will be faster if we talk" is a myth.

- A meeting that is a waste of time is an email **that would never have been written**

- Talking is writing with less effort & thought

- "Fast" for you, is a tax on everyone else's time

## **3. Meetings are for discussions**

“Most people” use meetings for discussion.

Which leads to…

- Questions

- "What about..."

- Ending in the same place you started

What “most people do” doesn’t work.

So do something else. Meet to resolve conflicts or coordinate closely when needed.

## **4. Meetings are for making decisions**

99% of the time, this is a giant mistake.

Al Pittamplalli has a helpful framework for making decisions without having pointless meetings.

- Frame the decision

- Require informed participants

- Do not confuse meetings with the actual decision-making process
