# 2 Steps Professionals Can Use To Save 5 Hours This Week

**In today’s work environment, we often find ourselves stacked in back-to-back meetings, many of which seem to have no value.**

I’ve used a two-step script to tactfully and effectively decline unnecessary meetings, reclaiming precious time for impactful work.

## Step 1: The Qualification Check

**The first step involves qualifying the necessity of the meeting and your role in it.**

Send a message like:

"Hi \[Name\], I hope you're well. Can you answer two quick questions about this meeting?

1\. What decision do we need to make in this meeting?

2\. Am I required to make any of those decisions? Thanks!"

This approach cuts straight to the meeting's purpose. It’s not about being dismissive but understanding the value you can (or can’t) add. You're not just another attendee; your time has value.

## Step 2: The Respectful Decline

**If it turns out your participation isn’t essential, it’s time to bow out gracefully:**

"Thanks for the info! I have some pressing work right now. Could you send me the key points and any decisions post-meeting?"

**This is polite yet assertive.**

You’re not simply rejecting the invitation; you're respecting the meeting's objective while prioritizing your work. This isn’t just about declining meetings but smart time management.

This script isn't about dodging meetings; it's a practice for your professional autonomy. You’re choosing to devote your time to work that warrants your attention. So, the next time that meeting invite pops up, remember this script.

**You'll be doing yourself a huge favor.**
