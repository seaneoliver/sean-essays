# Your Network Is Your Key to the Hidden Job Market

**Here's a wake-up call from 2023 stats showing 70% of jobs are filled through networking, not the usual job applications.**\
\
This isn't just a statistic; it's a crucial insight into the job market. It's clear: building a network isn't enough. You must engage actively with it.

**Networking isn’t about collecting contacts.**

It’s about forging genuine relationships. Research backs this up - those who keep in touch regularly and meaningfully with their network are far more likely to find job opportunities. What does this look like?

**It's more than just the occasional "checking in" email.**

It involves sharing relevant updates and useful insights and actively helping others succeed in your circle. Think of your network as a garden. Like any garden, it thrives on regular care and attention.

**This means consistently watering it with contributions, knowledge, and support, not just when you need something.**

Each interaction should add value. Engage in discussions, offer your skills, and connect people. This not only strengthens your network but also reinforces your role as a valuable, active member.

**The benefits are mutual.**

Investing in others’ success solidifies your position as a trusted, go-to professional. This creates a powerful cycle of support and opportunities flowing both ways.

**Invest in your network like your career depends on it - because it does.** In today’s job market, who you know - and who knows you - can make all the difference.
