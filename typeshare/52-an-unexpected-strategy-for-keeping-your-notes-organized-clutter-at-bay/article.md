# An Unexpected Strategy for Keeping Your Notes Organized & Clutter at Bay

**Well-organized notes can make or break your productivity.**

I've made mistakes on my note-taking journey. Scattered notebooks. Scraps of paper. App after app unused.\
**My mistakes led me to 3 strategies for keeping my notes organized.**

## Strategy \#1: Use an Index

Books & libraries have them. Creating an index for your notes is a powerful way to point you to exactly what you are looking for. Use the keywords that are meaningful for you. Thank your librarian.

### Strategy \#2: Use a proven system

Any system. I'm biased and will recommend you buy some 3x5 cards and build a note-box. Whatever you choose, use some structure and stick to it.

### Strategy \#3: Pick a trusted place to keep your notes

This goes with \#2. Both HOW & WHERE go together. Notecards worked for me. You can use any tool, app, or approach from above, just pick ONE place and commit to it.

**My productivity easily doubled once I settled on a system through the 3 strategies above.**

When you work your way through these steps, you'll land on an approach that works for you.
