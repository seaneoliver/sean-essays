# 3 Tips To Avoid A Stale Career

**To avoid stagnation in your career, take ownership of your career path and make your next move.**

Three key tips can help you achieve this:

### Firstly, don't wait to be chosen. 

Make your career aspirations known to your manager and colleagues. Schedule a meeting with your manager to discuss your career goals and map out a plan to achieve them. By doing this, you'll avoid the mistake of assuming your manager or HR will automatically know what you want. For example, a colleague who expressed interest in taking on a new project was eventually given the opportunity to lead it.

### Secondly, expect resistance and choose yourself first. 

You might encounter pushback from others who want you to stay put. Don't let others dictate your career path. Prioritize your own needs and wants. For instance, a friend who faced resistance from colleagues when she expressed interest in a different role chose herself and pursued the opportunity, which ultimately led to a promotion.

### Lastly, seek out support. 

Support from colleagues, mentors, or coaches can help guide your next career move. They offer valuable feedback and advice and can even introduce you to new opportunities. Don't try to navigate your career journey alone. Identify individuals who can provide support and guidance, and schedule regular check-ins. For example, a colleague who received valuable career advice and guidance from a mentor ultimately landed a job offer at their dream company.

**Taking action is true ownership.**

Don't be afraid to make your next move and take control of your career path. By making your mission known, choosing yourself first, and seeking out support, you can avoid stagnation and propel your career forward.
