# 4 Skills I Want To Build On In 2024

1. Generative AI. **Learning how to apply AI to build custom GPTs for all of my mentorship & coaching was a valuable activity**. I also built a 2nd GPT as a personal knowledge base for my last job. Building bots like this is one of the most underrated ways to build leverage in your personal & professional life. More to uncover here.

2. Moderating small groups. **This year, I moderated a Job Search Council for a diverse group of job seekers.** Improving my facilitation skills is rewarding. Forming & participation in non-competitive groups will be a big part of my 2024. Diversity and inclusion is a daily practice.

3. Building autonomous agents. Back to AI, **learning how to make agents that do jobs has been exciting.** From brainstorming use cases, learning no-code and code-based solutions, and debugging, I got a chance to stretch myself. This is where the opportunities will be for folks who are prepared.

4. Learning query language. **Growing my skills to pull insight out of all the data I have around me has led to actionable insights**. From a catalog of book notes to a personal movie database, moving from collection to showing just-in-time information that is important to me is a skill I will continue to build. I've always learned from trying to do it myself at least once.
