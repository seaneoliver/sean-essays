# How to Set Up Your Note-Taking System Without These 3 Mistakes

A Zettlekasten can accelerate your personal and professional development. Unfortunately, I've made a few mistakes on my journey. **I'm sharing this here so you can avoid my mistakes.**

Get all the benefits while avoiding these three mistakes.

**Mistake \#1: Digital or Analog approach?** This can be a huge time suck. The fix is to pick ONE. **I go analog first.** Starting as simply as possible gets the hurdle of learning a new tool out of the way. You have the option of adding complexity later.

**Mistake \#2: Should I separate work & personal?** Another question that seems more significant than it is. You are one person. A Zettlekasten is a project for life. **There is little value in separating your knowledge artificially.**

**Mistake \#3: Why would I even want to do this?** Motivation is a foundational concern. This is a question best answered before you start. Do you want to:

- Learn faster?

- Build expertise?

- Improve your relationships?

Your answers will be the direction for your approach.

**You can avoid these mistakes and speed up your adoption of what can be your knowledge superpower.**
