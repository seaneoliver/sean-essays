# The 101 Guide To Adulting

**I'm going to share with you a method I've used to make managing life easier.**

"Life" is a word that covers much. Breaking it down into smaller chunks makes it easier to manage. With manageable pieces, you'll be able to make smart choices to move your life forward.

**Unfortunately, some people never get that far.**

### Most don't break their life into chunks

These are the simplest categories you can use, in no particular order:

- Personal development

&nbsp;

- Relationships

&nbsp;

- Contribution

&nbsp;

- Finances

&nbsp;

- Career

&nbsp;

- Health

&nbsp;

- Fun

These categories broadly represent most areas of life. Look at the seven categories above, which do you feel best about? What area would you like to improve?

**Here's how to find out.**

### Step 1. Score each category from 1-10

The highest scores are for the areas you feel best about. The lowest scores are areas you want to improve in.

### Step 2. Pick one area from your lowest scores

That's where you are going to start your work. Raising the quality of your life in these underperforming areas is how you dramatically improve your life. The high-scoring areas are places you can maintain.

**The low-scoring areas are where you can make the most impact.**

### Step 3. Experiment

You've surveyed your life and found a good target.

Now you can get creative and experiment. Doing anything different in these areas will yield different results. Try anything new.

**All these steps give you a chance to assess and act on your life in a deliberate way.**

You don't have to change your whole life, you can change a little part and get amazing results. Where will you start?
