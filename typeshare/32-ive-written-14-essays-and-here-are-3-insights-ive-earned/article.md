# I've Written 14 Essays And Here Are 3 Insights I've Earned

### Week 2 is done. I'd like to share some learnings.

### Data is one signal

**I've written** about points of view I strongly hold. **I've shared** experiences and lessons learned in an effort to be helpful. **I'm surprised every single time** by what the data reveals. I'm not pandering to stats, but I appreciate quantitative data.

**Data helps me learn and grow.**

More than just someone "liking" or enjoying what I wrote. I get a measure of what captured people's attention. I get a measure of what held it. I get feedback that helps improve my game.

**Playing the attention game**

This is about getting better at diverting attention for my own purposes. This can't be just for selfish aims. If you give me your attention you better be getting something back. It's nice to see that confirmation in messages and replies.

> **"In the end, it all comes down to discipline"-John Madden**

**I don't receive a parade with every essay I post.**

I post anyway. My hunch is that over time, all these externalized thoughts will accrue to something more meaningful than an individual atomic essay. The only way to confirm that is to stick to the fundamentals and keep posting.

Habits. Systems. Shipping matters.

What about you?
