# How I Fuel My Productivity With Snacks

**Most productivity content focuses on doing more.**

I'm going to talk to you about breaks. Specifically, snacks. The underrated recovery phase of work.

> "So gimme fuel, Gimme fire, Gimme that which I desire, Yeah!" - Metallica

You need fuel to get things done. Without the right juice, there is no "Go."

**It is during the rest periods that we recover.**

I'm convinced that this is the perfect time for a treat. Don't you deserve a break? What is a break without snacks?

I'm partial to:

- Candy

&nbsp;

- Chips

&nbsp;

- Beverages

### **Yes, candy is terrible for you.**

Get out of here with that negativity. It is my break. *Everything in moderation.*

My go-to candy winners:

- Dark side Skittles

&nbsp;

- Trolli sour gummy worms

The flavor profiles skew fruity for me. Fruity and sour. These are tiny pops of flavor that let me choose my taste adventure.

### **Sometimes I break with the sweet and opt for the salty.**

Chips of choice:

- Doritos-Cool Ranch

&nbsp;

- Doritos-Spicy Nacho

&nbsp;

- Doritos-Nacho

&nbsp;

- Chomps Grass-fed Beef sticks

Doritos count as 1. I added the beef sticks to get a little hit of protein, which helps with hunger. This is not nutritional advice.

### **When you're being productive, your thirst needs quenching.**

I try to \*cough\* limit my sugar by drinking water.

Best choices:

- Bubly Sparkling water - Strawberry

&nbsp;

- Ollipop - Orange Cream

&nbsp;

- Purified water

Life is short. Breaks from the hustle of 'Getting things done' is mandatory. These are the snacks that I use to get me through the day.
