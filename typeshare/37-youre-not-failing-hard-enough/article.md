# You're Not Failing Hard Enough

**Failure is a good thing.**

You can use it to your advantage. Follow me here. Pick a thing that you want to do.

**Something you have failed to do so far.**

Perfect. Here is the recipe for success. Are you ready?

**Fail in new ways.**

Commit to no longer repeating the specific ways you fail. Let me explain. If you are trying to wake up early, and setting an alarm for 5 am isn't working.

**The failure is the way you're trying to do it.**

Instead of setting an alarm at 5 am, try setting an alarm at 5 AND leaving your phone in the living room instead.

Mix it up.

Fail a NEW way. Make it a game. How many ways could you fail to wake up at 5 am before you accidentally found something that worked?

**The odds are on you stumbling into success.**

One new approach a week is 52 tries. That's a lot. One a DAY is 365. I double-dog dare you to fail 300+ times on purpose.

**I bet you can't.**

**I bet you win.** Try this mental inversion on something you are stuck on. List as MANY ways you can fail at it.

**Then, do the opposite.**
