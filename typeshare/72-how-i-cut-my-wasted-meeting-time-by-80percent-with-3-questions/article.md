# How I Cut My Wasted Meeting Time by 80% With 3 Questions

## Question 1: What's the purpose of this meeting?

**If you're like me, you've found yourself with a calendar packed with meetings.**

You've also noticed that many of these meetings were a total waste of time. The first line of defense is to always ask the organizer what the goal of this meeting is. It's a red flag if they fail to adequately answer that question.

**If they do provide a solid answer, move to question \#2.**

## Question 2: What decision needs to be made in this meeting?

**There are two good reasons for a meeting-making a decision, and resolving a conflict.**

Every other scenario doesn't require a meeting. I'll die on this hill. Everything else can be handled async.

**If there isn't a decision to be made, there is a near-zero chance you need to attend.**

## Question 3: Am I required to make this decision?

This is my favorite question.

Your mileage may vary, but 90% of the time you aren't going to be a required decision maker. The other 10% of the time you've identified up front that you are a key player. Use this information to make sure you're informed.

**But if you're not required, congrats, you've just avoided a meeting that will be an email you'll quickly delete.**
