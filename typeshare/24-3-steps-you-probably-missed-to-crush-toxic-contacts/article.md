# 3 Steps You Probably Missed To Crush Toxic Contacts 

**Not everyone in your circle is a true fan.**

You have contacts that are enemies in disguise. People keeping tabs on you with negative intentions. Clearing your network of these foes is critical. There is good news for you!

**You can find your network enemies with 3 simple tests.**

***Share a controversial opinion with your network.*** The more controversial, the better. People who support you will show curiosity. Detractors go straight to conflict.

**Support doesn't equal agreement.**

> *My personal favorite is, "I've learned a lot from Trump."*

**Remove people who ridicule you because they disagree with you.**

***Take an unusual action.*** Act in a way that is a departure for you. Do anything new.

Go vegan for a bit, or put bacon on everything. Once you share, note people's reactions. You’re looking to sample how people respond.

**True relationships don’t depend on sharing the same thoughts, opinions, and ideas.**

***Ask for advice.*** Ask for specific help or advice. Your fans will help you. Your enemies won't. Watch who responds with ridicule and scorn.

**People who focus on what they think you should do over what you have asked for help aren't for you.**

These actions give people a chance to help. Do they? Or do they tear you down?

**Reward the first. Remove the second.**
