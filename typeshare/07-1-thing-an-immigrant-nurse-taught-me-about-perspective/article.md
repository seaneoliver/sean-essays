# 1 Thing An Immigrant Nurse Taught Me About Perspective

**The lesson starts with a simple question, "How are you doing"?**

For most people, it's a polite courtesy. It's what you say before you get to what you really want to talk about. Nothing more than a pleasantry.

**The answer I hate the most is "Living the dream"!**

I hate it because it stinks of insincerity. When a co-worker responds this way I can feel myself get hot. THIS? THIS IS YOUR DREAM?

**Why waste my time with a trash answer to a genuine question?**

When I asked my nurse Stan how he was doing, he said "Pretty fantastic, living the dream" Most people say that & it's a lie. I believed him. He addressed my skepticism.

**For the next 3 minutes he shared:**

- Had his wisdom teeth removed with pliers in a garage(no anesthesia)

&nbsp;

- How his brother was pinned down and had his lymph nodes removed from his neck(not in a hospital)

&nbsp;

- That there are the dead, and those that would wish they could see another sunrise

&nbsp;

- How we have lived longer than those who died too young

&nbsp;

- How he came to the USA with his brother

&nbsp;

- How the sun hits our face today

&nbsp;

- How he & I had two feet

He shared these struggles with such warmth, strength, and appreciation for life at this moment that vs. his prior **life is a dream, and he's LIVING it.**

**Stan is the man.**

What about you? Are you living your dream? Do you appreciate how far you've come?

How quickly can you fully appreciate where you are RIGHT now?
