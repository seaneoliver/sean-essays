# 6 Things alumni shippers know that you don’t

A few folks are back for their 2nd, 3rd, and even 4th cohort of ship30for30.

There's wisdom there for those on their first adventure. I'm sharing 6 below. Like my mom said:

> "A word to the wise is sufficient"

### Ignore feedback

Feedback is your fuel for growth.

It’s valuable to receive. It’s helpful to give. Feed the feedback machine and you’ll get out what you put in.

### Pass on connection

There are over 400 shippers in this latest cohort.

That’s an amazing opportunity to connect with people who:

Share your interests

Are starting where you are

In the same \*cough\* boat as you

You’re short-changing yourself if you come out of this cohort without a single friend.

### Drift off course

Get distracted by other platforms.

What about Medium? What about Linkedin? What about a blog?

Don’t worry about running when your job is to walk.

### Ignore the fundamentals

Your priority is to write, and ship, for 30 days.

Until you do that tips and tactics don’t matter. Scheduling tools don’t matter. Analytics don’t matter.

### Skip the course materials

There is a gold mine of valuable content provided.

It’s there to help you write better. Videos, min-courses, templates. You’d be cheating yourself by not leveraging this value.

### Quit

Writers write.

Quitters quit. Buying a course doesn’t magically get you the result. If you want the noun, you’ll need to do the verb.
